The Meridian Heist: a three-job stealth robbery game. Sneak past patrolling guards and sweeping security cameras, lift paintings, gold and diamonds, crack wall safes with a lockpick, and reach the getaway before the alarm tops out — across a museum gallery, a bank vault and a casino penthouse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD or arrows to move, Shift to sneak, click the floor to walk there, Q for a smoke bomb, E for an EMP burst, hold F by a safe to crack it, P to pause.
